Forwarding chooses the longest matching network prefix. A /32 host route can beat a /24 route even with a larger metric. Metrics are compared only among equally specific prefixes in this workbench.

IPv4 and IPv6 use separate tries. Subnet counts are exact integers, returned as decimal strings to preserve values beyond JavaScript’s safe-number range. Counts describe addresses, not usable hosts; IPv6 has no broadcast address.
